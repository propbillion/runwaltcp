When is RUNWAL THE CENTRAL PARK launching? +

The Sky Club Edition launches in 2026. Pre-launch bookings are open now to a select set of invited patrons. Pre-registered buyers receive priority allocation, launch-phase pricing, and a ₹1 Lakh discount.

Where is the project located? +

Runwal The Central Park is located on Old Mumbai-Pune Highway, Pimpri-Chinchwad — near Ranka Jewelers. Minutes from Chinchwad Metro, MIDC, IT hubs, and the industrial corridor, with seamless connectivity to Hinjewadi, the airport, and central Pune.

What residence types are available? +

2 BHK Large, 3 BHK Large, 3.5 BHK, 4 BHK Queen, 4.5 BHK King, and the signature 5 BHK Class — with carpet areas from 860 to 3100 sqft across 14 towers of 40–50 storeys.

How many amenities does the project offer? +

100+ lifestyle amenities, crowned by a 500-ft Sky Club on the 50th floor with R-Restaurant, infinity pool, US-standard gym, and pickleball court. 4 lakh sqft of recreational grounds. 55,000+ sqft delivered clubhouse — The R Club. 18 lifts. 70% open space across the 26-acre Master Community.

Who is the developer? +

Runwal Group — established 1978, one of Mumbai's premier real-estate organisations. 51 delivered projects across residential, commercial, and organised retail. Over 10,000 families happily resident across their developments.

Possession timelines vary tower-wise and are disclosed per MahaRERA registration (P52100025260). Detailed handover schedules are shared with serious buyers post pre-registration.
